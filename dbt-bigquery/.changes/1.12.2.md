## dbt-bigquery 1.12.2 - November 04, 2026

### Fixes

- Retry a query with a fresh job_id when the previous attempt's job failed terminally, instead of reattaching to the dead job and returning the same error. Reattachment is still used when the existing job is running, already succeeded, or is a multi-statement script that may have partially committed, so retries never duplicate committed work. ([#](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt-adapters/issues/))
- On a 409 Conflict, attach to the existing BigQuery job instead of resubmitting it. A terminally failed job is retried by the retry layer under a job_id of its own, so job_retries is honored for multi-statement scripts too. ([#2006](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt-adapters/issues/2006))
